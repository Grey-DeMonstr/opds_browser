# Privacy Policy

**App:** OPDS Browser (`monster.greyde.opds_browser`)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-22

## Summary

OPDS Browser does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

There are no accounts, no login, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Nothing
is sent anywhere except to the OPDS catalogue servers you choose to add.

## What is stored, and where

Everything the app stores stays on your device:

- The address and display name of each OPDS catalogue you add, plus two
  preferences controlling how downloaded files are organised into folders.
- A local database of book and folder metadata retrieved from those catalogues,
  used as a cache so browsing stays fast offline.
- The books you download, written to a folder you pick yourself through the
  Android system folder picker. The app can only write inside that folder.

Uninstalling the app removes the settings and the local database. Downloaded
books remain in the folder you chose, because that folder belongs to you and
not to the app.

## Network access

The app connects only to:

- The OPDS catalogue servers whose addresses you enter.
- Whatever image hosts those catalogues name for their cover images.

Those servers are operated by third parties and have their own privacy
practices, which this policy does not cover. The app sends them nothing beyond
the requests needed to list and download the content you ask for.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to reach the catalogues you configure.
- **Access to a folder you select** — granted through the Android Storage
  Access Framework, so downloads can be written. The app has no access to any
  other part of your storage.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
