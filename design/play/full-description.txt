OPDS Browser is a small, focused client for OPDS catalogues — the open standard that public-domain libraries, personal Calibre servers and community archives use to publish their collections. Supports OPDS 1.x without authentication only.

Capabilities:
• Browse folder hierarchies with caching
• Download an entire folder (author or series)
• Bookmark favourite folders for one-tap access from the home screen
Note: you need a separate ebook reader app to read what you download. OPDS Browser finds, downloads and organises books; it does not open them.
